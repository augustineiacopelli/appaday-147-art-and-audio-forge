  Engine: Phase 2 engine code lives in src/engine-sprites.js; build.js splices it into ENGINE:RENDER above the freeze line

  Composer: a raster of cell codes (material 1 outline, 2 to 6 the five ramps in slot order, 7 accent alias, 8 ground
    shadow; high nibble a fixed step) and layer ids. Generators draw in unit coordinates scaled by tileSize / 16 (sprites,
    32 for enemies, 48 for portraits, 16 for icons), so every size rasterizes natively. Shading: top and left edges of a
    region (one material in one layer) light, bottom and right shade, a later layer on top casts shade; bands widen with
    scale. Outline from the alpha mask; the outer pixel ring is reserved so it always fits. Slots resolve through the
    shared 16 slot ramp positions (2-4, 5-7, 8-10, 11-13, 14-15) for every layout. Left mirrors right. Ground shadow is a
    dither in slot 1. Two color masters: colorways map step 0 dark and the rest light (engine palette.colorway).
  Humanoid: body plans slight, average, broad, small, tall define the skeleton (the body part is the anchor source); poses
    are parameter sets (Phase 2: stand, stepA, stepB, idle; Phase 3 adds entries). Layer order per direction; up puts front
    gear behind and back gear on top. Default library: 5 bodies, 3 heads, 7 hair, 6 torsos, 4 legs, 3 back, 6 front, and
    one body per enemy rig (humanoid, quadruped, avian, serpent, ooze, construct, floater, plant): 42 prt_ records.
  Records: prt_ {layer, rig, lib, gen {shape, params} | px {base, variants {"<pose>.<dir>": {w, h, d}}}}. spr_ {kind
